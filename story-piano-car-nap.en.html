A little moment remembered by Dad

Dad, I just had a little nap

Everyday momentsHis words

Story date not yet recorded

See this day →

Yang Yu was our second child. He was only six, and I still find it hard to describe his personality in a few words. I can only tell some little stories.

He liked joining in conversations, and he liked repeating what other people said. I felt that sometimes he was giving himself a little time to think.

His piano lessons were around two in the afternoon. Whenever I drove him to class, he would fall asleep in the car. When we arrived, whether he woke up on his own or I woke him, he would instinctively say, “Dad, I just had a little nap.”

I felt he might be worried I would be annoyed. I knew him, so I played along, talked about something else, and coaxed him into class.
