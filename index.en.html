2:00PM
On the way to piano class

A little moment Dad remembers

“Dad,
I just had a little nap.”

His piano lessons were around two in the afternoon. Dad remembers that whenever he drove Yang Yu to class, Yang Yu would fall asleep in the car.

When they arrived, whether he woke up on his own or Dad woke him, he would instinctively say these words.

Dad played along, talked about something else, and coaxed him into class.
