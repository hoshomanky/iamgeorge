关于 IAMGEORGEABOUT IAMGEORGEFROM DAD

我是洋芋的爸爸。George Fang 的中文名是方智灏，家里叫他洋芋。IAMGEORGE 是我为他慢慢建立的一个数字世界。我想让来到这里的人，通过他的画作和日常的小故事，认识他。I’m George’s dad. His full name is George Fang, 方智灏 in Chinese. At home, we called him Yang Yu. IAMGEORGE is a digital world I am slowly building for him. I want people who visit to get to know him through his drawings and stories from everyday life.

01

他喜欢的颜色The colors he loved

洋芋喜欢五彩缤纷的颜色，也喜欢那些一张上有许多彩色小图形的贴纸。他画的东西很小，画风很稳。我希望这里也丰富、快乐，有他喜欢的颜色和小小的图形。

页面上的彩色小图形是网站装饰。洋芋自己的作品，会单独注明。

Yang Yu loved bright colors. He also liked stickers filled with lots of tiny, colorful shapes. He drew very small things, with a steadiness in his style. I want this place to feel colorful and happy, with the colors and little shapes he liked.

The colorful shapes around these pages are website decorations. George’s own artwork will be clearly identified.

02

六年的生活Six years of life

他只有六岁，我还很难用几个词说清他的性格。我能讲的是那些记得的故事。

我有很多他的照片，也想把照片背后的日常记下来。我们会按发生的时间，慢慢整理这六年的照片和文字，再放入相关的视频与录音。

有些往事会在整理时想起来，有些会在很久以后突然记起，都可以再补进去。记不清的日期和细节会如实说明，已有的故事也会随着新资料继续补充。

He was only six. I still find it hard to describe his personality in a few words. What I can share are the stories I remember.

I have many photographs of him, and I want to record the everyday moments behind them. We will gradually arrange photographs and writing from his six years in chronological order, alongside related video and audio recordings.

Some memories will come back as we sort through them. Others may return much later. They can all be added. We will be honest about dates and details we cannot remember, and update stories as more material becomes available.

03

真实的记忆，认真保存Caring for the real memories

我的原始讲述会完整保存。网站里的文字会经过微调，也有英文版本。孩子的原话和家人的观察、理解，会清楚区分。

AI 可以帮助整理、翻译和查找资料。经过 AI 处理或演绎的部分会明确标注，原始照片、作品和录音另行保存。没有留下记录的事情，我们会保留未知，不替洋芋编写他的想法。

每一项内容是否公开，都由家人决定。有些回忆可以与你分享，有些只留在家里。

My original accounts will be kept in full. The writing on this website will be lightly edited, with an English edition as well. We will clearly distinguish George’s own words from our family’s observations and interpretations.

AI may help us organize, translate and find material. Anything processed or reinterpreted with AI will be clearly labeled. Original photographs, artwork and recordings will be preserved separately. Where there is no record, we will acknowledge what we do not know and will not invent George’s thoughts.

Our family decides what to make public. Some memories can be shared with you. Others will stay within the family.

04

谢谢你来认识洋芋Thank you for getting to know Yang Yu

这个网站会慢慢补充。我希望把它长期维护下去，让多年以后来到这里的人，仍然能够认识洋芋。

以后，我也希望这里能给同样失去孩子的家庭一点陪伴。我们按自己的节奏，继续记录。

谢谢你愿意在这里停留。

We will keep adding to this website. I hope to maintain it for many years, so that people who visit in the future can still get to know Yang Yu.

In time, I also hope this place may offer some companionship to other families who have lost a child. We will keep recording, at our own pace.

Thank you for spending some time here.
