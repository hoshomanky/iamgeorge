去上钢琴课的路上

爸爸记得的一个小片段A little moment Dad remembers

“爸爸，
我刚眯了一会儿。”“Dad,
I just had a little nap.”

钢琴课大约在下午两点。爸爸记得，每次开车送洋芋去上课，他都会在车上睡着。His piano lessons were around two in the afternoon. Dad remembers that whenever he drove Yang Yu to class, Yang Yu would fall asleep in the car.

到了地方，无论是自己醒来，还是爸爸喊他，他都会下意识地说这句话。When they arrived, whether he woke up on his own or Dad woke him, he would instinctively say these words.

爸爸配合他打马虎眼，聊点别的，再哄着他去上课。Dad played along, talked about something else, and coaxed him into class.
