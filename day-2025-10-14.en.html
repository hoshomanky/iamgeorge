Looking up at Mum’s phone

On the morning of 14 October 2025, Mum was taking Yang Yu to school and filmed this short clip in the lift.

Yang Yu was wearing a red jacket and had his schoolbag on his back, standing beside Mum. As Mum held up her phone to film them in the mirror, he moved a little closer, leaned over and tilted his head up to look at the phone.

He looks so sweet with his neck stretched up like that.
