A day as a kindergarten helper

On 13 October 2025, Yang Yu was on duty as a helper at kindergarten.

Two children take on this role each day. They check the temperatures of children arriving at kindergarten and help them wash and sanitise their hands.

In the photos, Yang Yu is wearing red clothes and a blue sash, holding a thermometer to check another child’s temperature.

Yang Yu started in the youngest class. Now he is in the oldest class and will soon be finishing kindergarten. Looking at these photos of him on duty, I think he looks very confident.

That evening, Yang Yu sat in a chair with a book open in his hands, smiling happily.
